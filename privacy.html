Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the applications made by Amir Group B.V., Haarlem, the Netherlands (KvK 72823984). Contact: amir.nogayev@gmail.com.

What data we access

With your permission, the application accesses your Google Calendar data through the Google Calendar API: calendars, events, event times, titles, descriptions, locations and attendees. We access only the data that is necessary for the features you use.

How we use the data

We use calendar data only to show, plan and manage your own calendar inside the application. We do not use it for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not share it with third parties, except when the law requires it.

Google API Services

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any cached calendar data are stored only as long as necessary to operate the application. We protect them with access controls and encryption in transit.

Your rights

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can ask us to delete your data, or ask for a copy of it, by email. Under the GDPR you can also file a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes
