Hi, I'm Amir. I started as a developer in Kazakhstan, did my master's in China, and ended up in Haarlem building companies. I'm a father of two boys.

Today most of my time goes into one question: what happens to a company when agents do the work and people make the decisions? We don't just talk about it, we run our own company this way.

Now

  • Dark Office. Like dark kitchens and dark stores, but for an office. Agents handle admin, support, bug fixes and part of sales. A human stays in the loop for decisions. The surprising part: agents need management, just like people. Roles, policies, trackers, reviews.
  • Personal agents. On top of the office I run my own agent. It reads our dashboards, chats, email and error reports, and tells me in one chat what needs my attention.
  • New Clients. Agents for sales and outreach. newclients.ai

What I care about

  • LinkedIn automation. Since 2020 I've co-run Famelab, where we manage LinkedIn strategy for clients. I know this platform from the inside, from the scraping engine to the sales funnel.
  • AI that actually does the work. Not chat, but agents with clear rules about what they can and can't do. My advice is always the same: start with what really hurts, approve every action by hand first, and automate only what you keep approving.
  • Complex systems. I like it when many moving parts work together and nobody needs to watch them.

  • A homelab, set up and managed with Ansible.
  • 3D printing, mostly small practical things for the house.
  • Family history. Researching my family across the former USSR, archive by archive.
